About Central Pharmacy

Central Pharmacy is a community-focused pharmacy that combines a professional dispensary with everyday health and wellbeing products. This website is a student-built prototype of how customers might shop, send a prescription for review, and choose delivery or collection.

What we stand for

  • Clear, calm advice from pharmacists
  • Accessible shopping for the whole family
  • Reliable collection and delivery options
  • Respect for privacy — this demo never stores real medical files

The team
